---
title: "How Satoshi Perps Work"
description: "A brief overview"
---

Satoshi Perps is a decentralized perpetuals trading platform built on the Core network. Satoshi Perps pays all profits out in SolvBTC.CORE and is fully backed by BTC while allowing other Collaterals for trading. Satoshi Perps allow you to leverage your capital into long and short positions while maintaining your exposure to BTC
