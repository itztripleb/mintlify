title: "Acquiring SolvBTC.CORE"
---

1. Buy BTCB on supported Dexes on the BNB Chain / Mint BTCB by withdrawing BTC to a BEP20 wallet 2. Swap BTCB to SolvBTC.b on Solv Protocol (https://app.solv.finance/solvbtc?network=bnb-chain) 3. Bridge SolvBTC.b from the BNB chain to Core via free bridges

- Free Tunnel (https://tunnel.free.tech/solvbtc.b)(Lock-mint bridge) Bridge duration: a few hours Bridge amount: minimum 1 SolvBTC.b Fees: gas fee only
- Free Express (https://app.free.tech/?token=SolvBTC) (Liquidity bridge) Bridge duration: a few minutes Bridge amount: no limit, depends on liquidity Fees: a small % fee and gas fee

4. Swap SolvBTC.b to SolvBTC.CORE on Solv Protocol (https://app.solv.finance/core) 

5.If you have SolvBTC.b, they can swap it to SolvBTC.CORE on BitFLUX too.https://bitflux.finance/swap​ 

6.You can simply swap $CORE for $SolvBTC.CORE through [AKKA](https://app.akka.finance/swap?chain=Core+Chain&outputCurrency=0x9410e8052Bc661041e5cB27fDf7d9e9e842af2aa)
