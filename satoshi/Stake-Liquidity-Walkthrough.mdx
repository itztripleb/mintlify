# Step-by-Step Guide

## Funding Your Wallet

Follow the guide in [**How to Trade**](https://satoshiapp.mintlify.app/satoshi/How-to-Trade).

## Connecting Your Wallet

Connect via the Satoshi Perps dAPP using the “Connect Wallet” button.

![Slss1 Pn](/images/slss1.png)

## Navigate to Stake Liquidity

Click “Stake Liquidity” on the trade tab.

![Slss2 Pn](/images/slss2.png)

## Choose Your Asset

Select the asset you want to stake (e.g., SolvBTC). Click the asset you would like to stake and then click the “Stake with \___\_” button next to the asset

For todays example we will be using SolvBTC

![Slss3 Pn](/images/slss3.png)

## Choose the Amount to Stake

Input the amount in the Stake SLP tab.

![Slss4 Pn](/images/slss4.png)

Approve the asset and confirm in your wallet.

![Slss5 Pn](/images/slss5.png)

And confirm the approval in your wallet.

![Slss6 Pn](/images/slss6.png)

Next click the "Stake SLP" button.

![Slss7 Pn](/images/slss7.png)

And lastly confirm in your wallet and you're officially staked\\\\!\
![Slss10 Pn](/images/slss10.png)

## Viewing Your Stake

Your balance is displayed on the dashboard.

![Slss9 Pn](/images/slss9.png)
