### **Staking**

Staking Liquidity is straight forward, once you navigate to the Staking page, You'll notice the staking form on the right

Once you're here:

- Choose the asset you want to stake from the dropdown list to the top right
- Enter the amount you want to stake
- Approve the asset you want to stake and confirm in your wallet
- Once you're approved, click the stake SLP button and confirm in your wallet
