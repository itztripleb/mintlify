title: "Setting up Your Wallet"
description: "How to get a wallet to use with Satoshi Perps"
---

To do anything on Satoshi Perps, you will need an EVM wallet, you can use any wallet but for this tutorial I will walk you through using Metamask.

- First visit https://metamask.io/download and install the extension on your browser of choice, or get the mobile app
- Click the "Add to [your browser]" button
- On Popup, click "Add Extension"
- After the app or extension is installed there will be setup steps that are easy to follow
