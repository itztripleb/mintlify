---
title: "What are Perps?"
description: "Your brief introduction to perpetual contracts"
---

Perpetual contracts, or "perps," are a type of derivative that lets traders speculate on the price movements of an underlying asset without the constraints of an expiration date.

Unlike traditional futures contracts, perps continuously track the asset’s spot price through funding mechanisms that balance long and short positions. This design enables high leverage, flexible trading strategies, and efficient capital use, making them a popular choice in decentralized finance.
