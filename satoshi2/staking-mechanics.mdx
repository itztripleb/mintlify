title: "Staking Mechanics"
---

### **Staking and Unstaking Duration**

There is no minimum duration for staking, however the longer you stake the more rewards you will potentially earn.

### **Rewards & Distribution**

All yield is auto compounded in the vault directly, users do not have to claim any fees, their staking position just grows based off traders net losses and fees paid.

### **Lock-up Periods**

There is no lock-up period for staking however staking and quickly unstaking may result in a small loss in funds due to fees.

### **Security**

We deeply care about the safety of our users and the integrity of the system. Like any smart contract based platform, one of the primary risks lies in the potential compromise of administrative privileges. If these keys were to fall into the wrong hands, malicious actors could attempt to push unauthorized upgrades to the contracts.

To mitigate this, we've implemented a robust security measure: all administrative actions, including upgrades, are gated behind a 3 out of 5 multisig wallet. This means that no single individual has the power to act unilaterally any significant change requires consensus from a trusted majority.

Security is not just a feature it's a priority. We’re continuously reviewing and improving our infrastructure to ensure the safest possible experience.
