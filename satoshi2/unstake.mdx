---
title: "How to Unstake"
---

To unstake on Satoshi Perps Switch from the staking tab to the unstaking at the top of the staking form, after you've done this:

- Choose the asset you used before to stake
- Enter the amount you would like to unstake, or click the number next to the text saying "Available" if you want to unstake the full amount
- Press the "Unstake SLP" button
- Sign the transaction in your wallet

And now you've unstaked from Satoshi Perps
