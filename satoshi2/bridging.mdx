title: "Bridging Assets"
description: "Getting on to the Core network"
---

Bridging to Core is easy and their native bridge supports most popular chains such as Ethereum, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Optimism. To bridge:

- Head to https://bridge.coredao.org/
- Press the "Go to transfer" button
- Select the chain your bridging from
- Select the asset (we recommend $$USDC or $$

  USDT as they have the most liquidity on Core)
- Enter the amount you would like to bridge (you will want to bridge at least $$20 USD worth and then Core will provide you with 0.2 $$

  CORE to cover your first transaction)
- Press the bridge button

Once the transaction has processed you have finished bridging to Core.
